Word toggle

The highlighted words in Post’s sentence: “have” or “need”, “a space” or “a thing”.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Post opens with one sentence, “I have a thing to rent out”, and the member changes its highlighted words. Each pair is a radio group drawn as words in the headline. “have” makes a listing for rent and turns orange; “need” makes a wanted listing and turns blue; the kind is ink.

Use it only in that sentence. Any other choice is a radio group or the switch.

Anatomy

  1. SentenceThe screen’s h1, in the display face.
  2. Chosen wordFilled: orange for “have”, blue for “need”, ink for the kind.
  3. Other wordMuted, on the track, and still readable.
  4. Rest of the sentenceChanges with the choice: “to rent out.” or “to rent.”

Responsive behaviour

The sentence is 36 px on a phone and 48 px from 768 px. The pairs wrap as whole units, so a pair is never split across lines.

Theming

Word toggle reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-bg-subtle, --color-fg-muted, --color-accent, --color-fg-on-accent, --color-rent, --color-fg-on-rent and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="radiogroup" with a name, holding native radios in labels. A screen reader hears “Do you have it or need it? have, radio button, checked, 1 of 2”.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Moves to the chosen word of each pair.
← → Switch the word.
Space Chooses the focused word.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

Each group has an aria-label that asks the question. Both words stay visible, so the state is carried by the radio’s checked state and by position, not by colour alone.

Motion

The fill moves between words over --duration-base. Reduced motion makes it instant.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • The words are fixed: “have”, “need”, “a space”, “a thing”.
  • The sentence ends “to rent out.” for “have” and “to rent.” for “need”.
  • Switching keeps every field already filled in; choosing “need” removes the photos field.
