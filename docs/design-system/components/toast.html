Toast

A short message that rises from the corner of Admin after a decision.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Toasts are for Admin, where a decision removes the item from view and the admin needs to know it took. On the public screens the result appears where the member is looking (a confirmation in place), so they have no toasts.

Anatomy

  1. IconSays the tone along with the title.
  2. TitleWhat happened.
  3. TextOn what, and what follows.
  4. DismissA 44 px button.

Variants

We couldn’t reach christian.rent.
Info, success, warning, danger

Sizes

Toast has one size.

States

A toast is shown or gone. At most three stack, with the newest at the bottom; a fourth replaces the oldest that would dismiss itself. One that dismisses itself draws a bar along its foot (toast__progress) that runs out over 8 seconds and stops while the pointer or the focus is on it.

Responsive behaviour

Under 640 px the region spans the bottom of the screen. From 640 px it is 24 rem wide in the bottom right corner.

Theming

A toast is --color-bg-inverse in both themes, so it stands apart from the page.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Toasts appear in a role="status" region that is in the page before any toast arrives; a failure is a role="alert".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Reaches the action and Dismiss.
Enter Activates them.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

Title and text are read together.

Contrast

Text

Motion

It rises over --duration-base. It leaves after 8 seconds, pausing while hovered or focused. A failure never leaves by itself.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Title: the outcome in a word or two, “Approved”, “Removed”, “Already decided”.
  • Text: the item by name and what is now true.
  • There is no Undo: a decision is recorded and stands.
