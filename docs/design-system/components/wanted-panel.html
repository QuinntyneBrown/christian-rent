Wanted panel

The head of a wanted listing: where a photo would be, the request itself in white on blue.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

A wanted listing has no photo, so on the Listing screen its title takes the photo’s place. It is the large version of the wanted listing card.

Anatomy

  1. Tag“Wanted · thing” or “Wanted · space”.
  2. TitleThe screen’s h1.
  3. WhenWhen it is needed.

Sizes

Wanted panel has one size.

States

Not interactive: it has no states.

Responsive behaviour

From 576 px it keeps the photo’s 16:10 shape. Under 576 px it is as tall as its text needs.

Theming

Wanted panel reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-wanted, --color-fg-on-wanted. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A block whose title is the screen’s <h1>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
NoneNot focusable: it is read in document order.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

“Wanted” is written in the tag, so the panel never depends on its colour.

Contrast

Title and when

Motion

None.

Touch

Nothing to press.

Content

  • The title is the member’s own words, rendered as text and wrapped, never cut.
  • When: “Jul 14 to 18, 2026”, “Tuesdays and Thursdays”.
