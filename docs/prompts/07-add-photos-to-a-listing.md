# Feature: Add photos to a listing

Journey 7 of `docs/journeys.md` (Main): Add, check and remove photos while posting a listing.

## Goal

While posting a listing for rent, a member adds photos, sees each uploading and then in the preview, removes
one, and is told when a photo is refused or when the limit is reached. Photos are checked for type, size and
dimensions before they are stored, are served from storage, and appear on Browse and the Listing screen once
the listing is approved. A wanted listing has no photos. Today Post (journey 6) has no photo upload; serving a
rendition exists from journey 2.

### In scope

- `POST /api/photos` (member only): validation of type, size and dimensions before storage, renditions produced, stored in file storage; `DELETE /api/photos/{id}` for the member's own unattached photo; attaching photos on `POST /api/listings`.
- An unattached or another member's photo is never served or deletable by anyone else (refused, revealing nothing).
- `cr-photo-picker` on Post with the photo-uploading, photo-refused and photos-full states; object URLs revoked and uploads aborted on remove or leave; the picker loaded lazily.
- Photos shown in the live preview, on Browse and on the Listing screen.

### Deferred (not in this run)

- Reordering photos: no mock exists (open decision). Do not build it.
- Cropping or editing a photo.
