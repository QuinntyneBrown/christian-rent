Dialogs and overlays

When something opens over the page, and how it behaves.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview

  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive behaviour
  • Accessibility

Overview

There are three dialogs and no other overlay: no drawers, popovers, menus or tooltips. Each dialog asks one thing.

When to use

DialogOpens fromAsks
Report a listingReport, on ListingA reason from a fixed list and an optional note.
Confirm RemoveRemove, in AdminWhether to remove the named listing or member.
Become a memberPost, a message or a report, when the person is not a memberA name, a church and the declaration.

Structure

Remove “Party bus, cheap, DM for details”?

The listing stops being public, every open report on it is closed, and Mike Rentals is told it was removed.

A destructive confirmation.

States

Just opened, invalid, busy, failed, and for the report, sent and already reported. A failure keeps the inputs and offers “Try again”.

Responsive behaviour

A bottom sheet under 640 px, centred from there. Never more than one dialog at a time.

Accessibility

  • Focus is trapped; the page behind is inert.
  • Escape and Close leave without acting; focus returns to the button that opened it.
  • A destructive dialog starts on the safe choice.
  • Built with CDK Dialog; never hand-rolled.

Content

  • The description says exactly what follows. For Remove: the listing stops being public, open reports on it close, the member is told, and who decided is recorded.
