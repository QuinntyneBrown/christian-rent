Queue item

One thing waiting in Admin’s review queue: a new listing, a report or a new member.

Overview

The queue lists what waits, oldest first. Each item says what it is, who it is from, how long it has waited and, in a tag, which of the three kinds it is.

Anatomy

  1. ThumbnailThe listing’s photo; “Want” on blue for a wanted listing; initials for a member.
  2. TitleThe listing’s title or the member’s name; one line, cut with an ellipsis.
  3. From and ageWho posted or which church, and how long it has waited.
  4. KindA tag: New, Reported or New member.

Responsive behaviour

The queue is the whole screen under 768 px, where there is no detail to open: each item is drawn without a button and none is current. From 768 px it is a column of at most 448 px beside the detail, and each item is a button.

Theming

Queue item reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-fg-muted, --color-bg-subtle, --color-border-default, --color-bg-surface-sunken, --color-wanted, --color-fg-on-wanted and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

From 768 px a <button type="button"> in a list; the open item has aria-current="true". Under 768 px plain text in a list. The mocks use links only so that their states can be reached.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Moves through the queue.
Enter Space Opens the item in the detail panel.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

The link reads title, from, age and kind. A cut title keeps its full text for assistive technology.

Contrast

Title
From and age

Motion

Colour and position changes take --duration-fast or --duration-base with --ease-standard. With reduced motion the tokens drop to 0.01 ms, so nothing moves.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Age is relative and short: “12 min”, “3 hr”, “2 days”.
  • The summary above the list counts: “4 waiting · oldest first”.
