Content and tone

How christian.rent sounds: plain, warm, Canadian, and true.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Overview

christian.rent speaks like a helpful person at church: plainly, kindly and without selling. It says only what it knows to be true. The full rules are in Content; this pattern shows them at work.

When to use

  • Use the product’s words and no synonyms: listing, for rent, wanted, space, thing, member, church, verified, message, review queue, report.
  • Canadian spelling: neighbourhood, colour, counselling.

States

Copy changes with state: an action names the person (“Message Dan”), a wait names the thing (“Loading listings”), a failure says what is safe.

Responsive behaviour

Copy is the same at every width. Nothing is abbreviated for a phone.

Accessibility

  • Meaning is in words, never in colour or an icon alone.
  • Dates cannot be misread: the month is a word.
  • The page language is en-CA.

Content

  • No payment words: no “book”, “reserve”, “checkout”, “deposit”.
  • No promises the product cannot keep: no reply times, no audience sizes.
  • The wordmark is lowercase “christian.rent”.
