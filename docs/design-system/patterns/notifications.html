Pattern

Notifications

How christian.rent tells someone that something happened.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • When to use
  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive behaviour
  • Accessibility
  • Content
  • Do and don’t
  • Sources

Overview

It tells them in place. A member sees a confirmation where the form was. An admin sees a toast because the item has left the screen. There is no notification centre, no badge and no banner.

When to use

MessageWhereLeaves
A listing was sent for reviewA confirmation in place of Post’s formWhen the member leaves
A message was sentA confirmation in place of the message boxWhen the member leaves
A report was sentInside the report dialogOn “Done”
An admin’s decision tookA toast in AdminAfter 8 s
An admin’s decision did not saveA toast in AdminOnly when dismissed

Structure

Approved
“Backyard garden for gatherings” is now public on Browse.
The one toast a decision produces.

States

Info, success, warning, danger; up to three stacked.

Responsive behaviour

Toasts span the bottom of a narrow screen and sit in the corner from 640 px.

Accessibility

  • A live region is in the page before the message arrives.
  • Failures are alerts and never dismiss themselves.
  • A timer pauses on hover and on focus.

Content

  • How a member learns of a reply to their message is not designed; nothing here promises an email or an inbox.
