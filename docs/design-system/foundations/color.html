Foundation

Color

Plain white, cool grey surfaces and hairlines, navy ink, and two accents that mean something: orange for what is for rent, blue for what is wanted.

Overview

The interface is white. Surfaces and hairlines are a cool grey called mist; text and the plain primary action are ink. Never beige or cream.

Two accents carry meaning. Orange (sun) is anything offered for rent and the brand. Blue (sky) is anything wanted. Green (leaf) is verified, approved and sent. Red is removal and failure. Because colour carries meaning, it is always backed by a word or an icon.

Usage

  • Read a role (--color-rent), never a primitive and never a hex.
  • Orange as a fill takes ink text (--color-fg-on-rent). Orange as text is --color-fg-rent; large display text may use --color-fg-rent-display. The brand orange itself is text only in the wordmark.
  • Blue as a fill takes white text in both themes.
  • Do not use orange or blue for anything that is neither for rent nor wanted, and do not add a third accent.
  • To add a brand theme, add a [data-theme="name"] block that overrides semantic tokens only, then run the contrast check.
