Dialog

Overview

christian.rent never edits in place: a button that needs an answer opens a dialog. There are three, and each is one question. Anything longer is a screen. In the app this is a CDK Dialog; modals are never hand-rolled.

Anatomy

  1. Title and descriptionAn h2 that names the task and a line that says what will happen.
  2. CloseAn icon button; Escape does the same.
  3. BodyA few fields at most.
  4. FooterThe way out on the left, the action on the right.

Sizes

One width, 32 rem at most.

States

While busy the confirm button shows a spinner and ignores presses, and the way out is turned off. If the request fails, an alert appears at the top of the body, the inputs are kept and the button reads “Try again”.

Responsive behaviour

Under 640 px the dialog is a sheet fixed to the bottom of the screen, full width, with only its top corners rounded and its footer buttons sharing the row. From 640 px it is centred.

Theming

Dialog reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-bg-backdrop, --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-fg-default, --color-border-default, --color-danger-bg, --color-danger-icon and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA Dialog (modal): role="dialog", aria-modal="true", named by its title and described by its description. The page behind is inert.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Cycles inside the dialog only.
Esc Closes without acting.
Enter Submits the form.

Focus

Focus starts on the first field, or on the safe choice (“Keep it”) when the dialog confirms a removal. On close it returns to the button that opened the dialog.

Labelling

The title is the name. A destructive title names the thing: “Remove ‘Party bus, cheap, DM for details’?”

Motion

The panel scales in over --duration-slow; as a sheet it slides up. Reduced motion makes both instant.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • The title is the question or the task. The description says what follows, in full: who is told, what stops being public, what is recorded.
  • Buttons say what they do: “Send report”, “Remove listing”, “Keep it”, “Become a member”, “Not now”.
