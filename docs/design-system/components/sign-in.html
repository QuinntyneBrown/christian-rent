Sign in

The sign-in form of either application: a heading, an email and a password, Sign in, and what to do without an account.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

One form for both applications (L2-037, L2-038). In rent it sits on the page under the site header; in Admin it is sign-in--admin, inside the Admin gate’s card, with the overline “Admin”. It offers no way to create an account or reset a password, because neither is designed.

Anatomy

  1. HeadOverline (Admin only), the title and a line on who signs in here.
  2. MessageAn alert: refused, limited, unreachable or the session ended.
  3. FieldsEmail and Password, each a text field.
  4. Sign inA primary button the width of the form.
  5. Note and linkWho creates accounts, and the way out.

One column at every width, centred, at most 28rem wide. In Admin the card fills the width under 576 px, with 16 px around it.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <form> labelled by its title. Email is type="email" with autocomplete="username"; Password has autocomplete="current-password", so password managers fill them. Paste is never blocked and nothing is asked that relies on memory, a puzzle or a code (WCAG 2.2, 3.3.8).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabEmail, Password, Sign in, then the link.
EnterSends the form from either field.

Focus

Invalid: focus moves to the first empty field. Refused: the password is cleared and focus moves to it. The alert is announced (role="alert", or role="status" for the session that ended).

Labelling

Each field has a visible label; its error is tied to it with aria-describedby.

Contrast

Note

Motion

Only the busy button’s spinner, which stops under reduced motion.

Touch

Fields and the button are 44 px tall or more.

Content

  • Title: “Sign in”, or “Sign in to Admin”.
  • Refused: “That email and password don’t match an account.” Never say which was wrong or whether the account exists.
  • Limited: “Too many attempts from this device.” and when to try again.
  • No “Log in”, no “Forgot password”, no “Create an account”.
