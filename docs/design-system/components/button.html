Button

A button does something: it posts, sends, approves, removes. Its colour says what kind of thing it does.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use a button when pressing it changes something. Use a link when it only goes somewhere. The fill carries meaning on christian.rent:

  • For rent (orange) offers or answers something for rent: “I have something to rent”, “Publish listing”, “Message Dan”.
  • Wanted (blue) asks or answers a request: “Post request”, “Offer to help Marcus”, “Ask for …”.
  • Primary (ink) is the plain main action when neither applies: “Post”, “Search”, “Try again”, “Send report”.
  • Success (green) is an admin saying yes: “Approve”, “Keep listing”.
  • Danger removes; it appears only inside the confirmation. Danger quiet opens that confirmation.
  • Outline, secondary, ghost and link are for everything of less weight.

Anatomy

  1. Icon (optional)1.25em, the label’s colour, aria-hidden.
  2. LabelA verb phrase in sentence case. It is the accessible name.
  3. ContainerA pill (--radius-full), 44, 48 or 56 px tall, with a 1 px border in the fill’s colour.

Sizes

btn--sm in the header and in dense rows; the default elsewhere; btn--lg for Publish on Post.

States

Busy keeps the button’s width, hides the label behind a spinner and ignores further presses, so a listing or a message is never sent twice. Disabled is the real disabled attribute.

Responsive behaviour

Buttons keep their height at every width. In a dialog footer they share the row and grow to fill it under 640 px. On Browse the two buttons that lead to posting wrap onto two lines on a narrow phone.

Theming

Button reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-bg-surface, --color-fg-default, --color-border-strong, --color-bg-subtle, --color-bg-subtle-hover, --color-accent and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <button>, or an <a> carrying the same classes when it navigates.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter Activates.
Space Activates a button.
Tab Moves to the next control.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

The visible label is the name. An icon-only button has an aria-label that says what it does (“Close and go back to Browse”). A busy button has aria-busy="true".

Motion

Pressing scales the button to 97% for --duration-fast. Reduced motion removes it.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Start with a verb and say what happens: “Publish listing”, “Send report”, “Try again”. Never “OK”, “Submit” or “Yes”.
  • Name the person where there is one: “Message Dan”, “Offer to help Marcus”.
  • Sentence case, no full stop.
