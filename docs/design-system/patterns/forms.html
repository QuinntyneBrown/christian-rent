Pattern

Forms

How a member fills something in on christian.rent, and what happens when it goes wrong.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • When to use
  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive behaviour
  • Accessibility
  • Content
  • Do and don’t
  • Sources

Overview

There are four forms: Post, the message box, the report dialog and the join dialog. They share one behaviour so that a member learns it once.

When to use

  • A screen when the form is the point: Post.
  • A panel in the page when it sits beside what it is about: the message box.
  • A dialog when a button needs a short answer: report, join. Never an inline form that appears in place.

Structure

One column. A label above every control. Optional fields say “Optional”. One filled button at the end, with the fine print beside it.

What is it?
Prices are in CAD.
A line field, a form field and the action.

States

StateWhat the member sees
UntouchedExamples as placeholders; the preview shows a stand-in for each empty field.
InvalidOn submit every invalid field is marked at once, a summary lists them, focus moves to the summary, and nothing typed is lost.
SendingThe button is busy and cannot be pressed twice; fields are read-only; nothing moves.
FailedAn alert at the top; everything is kept, photos included; the button reads “Try again”.
Refused by a limitAn alert that says when the member can try again.
DoneThe form is replaced by a confirmation.

Responsive behaviour

Fields are one column at every width. Two short fields share a row only where the column is wide enough. On Post the live preview is beside the form from 768 px and below it on a phone. A dialog’s form becomes a bottom sheet under 640 px.

Accessibility

  • Every control has a visible label tied to it.
  • Errors are text under the field, tied with aria-describedby, and the field has aria-invalid.
  • The summary is announced and takes focus; its links move focus to each field.
  • The rules are checked again by the backend; the frontend’s checks are a convenience.
  • A person who is not a member is asked to join and returns with their choices kept.

Content

  • Errors say what to do and give the rule: “A price is between $0 and $100,000. Enter 0 to offer it free.”
  • Buttons say what they do: “Publish listing”, “Post request”, “Message Dan”, “Send report”.
