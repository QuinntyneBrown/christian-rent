Lists of listings and the queue

How Browse’s grid and Admin’s queue load, grow and stay bounded.

Overview

christian.rent has two lists and no tables. Browse is a grid of cards, newest first. The queue is a column of items, oldest first.

States

Loading, loaded, more loading, more failed (what is shown stays), end reached (no further request), empty, no results, failed.

Responsive behaviour

One, two, three and four columns of cards at 0, 576, 768 and 1200 px. The queue is the screen on a phone and a column from 768 px.

Accessibility

  • Both are real lists (<ul>), so their length is announced.
  • New items are added after the ones in view; focus never jumps.
  • Photos below the fold load lazily and have their space reserved.
  • Listings far off screen are released, so memory stays bounded however far a person scrolls.

Content

  • Counts are plain: “7”, “1,204”, “63 waiting · oldest first · showing 50”.
