Photo gallery

A listing’s photos: one large, the rest as thumbnails to step through.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

A listing for rent has up to six photos. The gallery shows one at a time and never opens a lightbox or a carousel that moves on its own. With one photo there is nothing to step through, so it shows no thumbnails and no count; with none, the photo’s place says “No photo yet”.

Anatomy

  1. Current photo16:10, --radius-xl.
  2. Current thumbnailAn ink border; aria-current="true".
  3. Other thumbnailsButtons that show their photo.
  4. Count“Photo 1 of 3”, announced when it changes.

Responsive behaviour

The photo fills the listing’s column. Thumbnails scroll sideways inside their row if there are more than fit.

Theming

Photo gallery reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-bg-surface-sunken, --color-fg-default, --color-fg-muted, --color-border-strong. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-label="Photos"> with a list of buttons.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Moves through the thumbnails.
Enter Shows that photo.
← → On a thumbnail: shows the photo before or after it and moves focus to its thumbnail.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

The large photo has an alt that says what it shows. Thumbnails have empty alt and a button name, “Photo 2 of 3”. The count is a polite live region.

Contrast

Count
Current thumbnail’s border

Motion

Colour and position changes take --duration-fast or --duration-base with --ease-standard. With reduced motion the tokens drop to 0.01 ms, so nothing moves.

Touch

Each thumbnail is 72 × 54 px. A swipe on the large photo also steps through.

Content

  • Alt text describes the photo: “A bright hall with round tables, folding chairs and tall arched windows”. Never “photo” or the file name.
