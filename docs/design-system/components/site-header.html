Site header

The bar at the top of Browse, Listing and Sign in: the logo, who is signed in with Sign out, or Sign in, and Post.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

The header takes you home, lets you post, and says who is signed in: the member’s initials and “Sign out”, or “Sign in” when nobody is. It has no menu and no Messages button, because neither is designed (L2-036).

Anatomy

  1. LogoLinks to Browse.
  2. MemberThe signed-in member’s initials. Not a control.
  3. Session control“Sign out”, or “Sign in” when nobody is signed in, which opens the sign-in page with this screen to return to.
  4. PostOpens Post.

Sizes

Site header has one size.

States

Its state is who is signed in, shown in the Variants: unknown (Post alone, so nothing flashes), signed out, signed in as a member, signed in and not a member. Its parts are a logo and buttons, whose own states apply; “Sign out” is busy while it is sent.

Responsive behaviour

The header is 64 px tall at every width. Below 400 px (24.99rem) “Sign in” and “Sign out” show their icon alone in a 44 px square, keeping their accessible name in .site-header__label. Below 360 px the initials are hidden, Post shows its word alone (.site-header__post-icon is hidden) and the actions close up to 4 px, so the logo, the session control and Post fit at 320 px.

Theming

Site header reads only semantic tokens. The colour tokens it reads are --color-bg-canvas, --color-border-default, --color-avatar-sky, --color-fg-on-avatar, --color-fg-default. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <header> landmark. It stays at the top while the page scrolls. “Sign in” is a link; “Sign out” is a button, since it changes state.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkip link, then logo, then Sign in or Sign out, then Post.
EnterFollows the link or presses the button.

Focus

The shared focus ring, never removed. After signing out, focus stays on the header’s new “Sign in”.

Labelling

The initials have the name “Signed in as Sarah Kim”. An icon-only session control keeps its word as its name.

Contrast

Initials
Post

Motion

None.

Touch

Every control is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min).

Content

  • “Sign in”, “Sign out” and “Post”. Never “Log in”, “Log out” or “Account”.
