Logo

The mark, a key whose head is a cross, and the lowercase wordmark with “.rent” in orange.

Overview

The logo is on every screen at every width, and it is the way home. The wordmark is always lowercase christian.rent; it is never “Christian Rent” or “ChristianRent” anywhere in the product.

Anatomy

  1. TileA rounded square in --color-brand-mark.
  2. KeyA cross whose upright runs down into two teeth.
  3. “christian”Bricolage Grotesque, extrabold.
  4. “.rent”The same face in --color-brand-mark.

Responsive behaviour

The logo does not change size. In Admin’s navigation between 768 and 991 px, where the rail is 72 px wide, the wordmark is hidden and the link keeps its name.

Theming

Logo reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-fg-default, --color-brand-mark, --color-fg-on-brand-mark, --color-fg-inverse. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Content

“.rent” in --color-brand-mark is 2.8:1 on white. That is allowed for a logo (WCAG 1.4.3 exempts logotypes) and for nothing else: orange text anywhere else uses --color-fg-rent or --color-fg-rent-display.

  • Clear space around the logo is at least the width of the mark’s cross arm.
  • The mark reads down to 16 px.
