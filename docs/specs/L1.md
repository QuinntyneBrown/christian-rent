# L1 - High-Level Requirements

christian.rent is where Christians post spaces and things for rent, or ask for a space or a
thing to rent. A space can be a room, a hall, a gymnasium, a locker or a parking space; a thing
can be a tool, a trailer or a set of chairs. No payment happens here; people agree the details
together.

The words used here are the ones the product uses: a **listing** is **for rent** or **wanted**,
and is for a **space** or a **thing**; a **member** belongs to a **church**; a member or a church
an admin has confirmed is **verified**; a member sends a **message** about a listing; an admin
works through the **review queue**, which holds new listings, new members and **reports**.

The mission (L0) is not written yet. When it is, it goes in `docs/specs/L0.md`.

## L1-001: Browse and search listings
Anyone must be able to browse the approved listings on the home screen, search them, switch
between Everything, For rent and Wanted, and limit them to spaces or to things. A search that
finds nothing must offer to post a wanted listing for it.

## L1-002: Read a listing
Anyone must be able to open one approved listing and see what it is, what it costs, when it is
available, roughly where it is, who posted it and which church they belong to, with the member
and the church each marked when verified.

## L1-003: Post a listing
A member must be able to post a listing in one short flow: say whether they have or need a space
or a thing, fill in a few fields, see a live preview of the card, and get a confirmation. A
listing covers any space or thing a person can rent, including a parking space, a locker or a
gymnasium.

## L1-004: Photos
A member must be able to add photos to a listing for rent. Photos must be checked before they
are stored and shown wherever the listing appears. A wanted listing has no photo.

## L1-005: Message the person who posted
A member must be able to send a message to the person who posted a listing, with dates when the
listing is for rent, and be told once it is sent. This is how a rental is arranged: the two
people agree the details together.

## L1-006: Members and churches
christian.rent is for Christians. A person must become a member, declaring that they are a
Christian and naming their church, before they post or send a message. An admin confirms members
and churches, and a confirmed member or church is shown as verified.

## L1-007: Review queue
An admin must be able to work through a review queue of new listings, reports and new members,
see the detail of each item, and Approve, Keep or Remove it. A listing is public only once it has
been approved. An empty queue says "All caught up".

## L1-008: Reports
A member must be able to report a listing that does not belong on christian.rent, and the report
must reach the review queue.

## L1-009: Launch in Canada
christian.rent launches in Canada: prices are in CAD, distances are in km, and the copy uses
Canadian spelling.

## L1-010: Narrow on purpose
christian.rent must take no payment, hold no booking calendar, and have no reviews, ratings,
social feed or adverts.

## L1-011: Security and privacy
The system must protect members and their data: the backend decides what each person may do,
everything a person submits is validated and treated as data, contact details stay private, and
abuse is rate limited.

## L1-012: Performance
The system must open fast on a phone on a slow connection and stay smooth however far a list is
scrolled.

## L1-013: Responsive on every screen
Every screen must work at every viewport width, from the narrowest phone to the widest desktop.

## L1-014: Accessibility
Every screen must meet WCAG 2.2 level AA and work by touch, by keyboard and with a screen reader.

## L1-015: Reliability and observability
The system must fail clearly and recoverably, lose nothing a member has written, and be
observable in production without recording personal data.

## L1-016: Sign in
A member must be able to sign in to christian.rent, and an admin to Admin, each on its own sign-in
page with its own session, and each must be able to sign out. Browsing, searching and reading a
listing must need no sign-in.

## L1-017: Two applications
christian.rent must be two applications, the site and Admin, each built and deployed on its own,
on its own origin, using one backend.

## Not yet decided

These are open, and the requirements above do not settle them:

- **How a person gets an account, and recovers it.** Sign-up, password reset, password change,
  email confirmation, sign-in with another provider and multi-factor sign-in are not designed.
  Until they are, an operator creates each account with `ChristianRent.Cli create-account`
  ([ADR 0029](../adr/0029-sign-in-and-two-sessions.md)), and nobody can create their own.
- **How a message reaches the person who posted.** A Messages inbox is not designed yet. L1-005
  requires that the message is delivered to that person and to nobody else; it does not say where
  they read it.
- **How a member manages what they posted.** A "My listings" screen is not designed yet, so
  editing and taking down one's own listing are not specified here.
- **How an admin removes a member who has left the review queue, and confirms a church none of
  whose members waits there.** The review queue holds new listings, reports and new members, and
  Admin has no other screen. An admin confirms a church from the detail of a new member of it
  (L2-018, criterion 7; ADR 0031); the mocks draw no control for the other two.
- **How a person's area is set, and how a distance is measured from it.** Browse and Listing show
  an area and distances in km; no screen sets the area.
- **How a member who posted is told that their listing was removed.** L1-007 requires that they
  are told; it waits for the Messages inbox. Until then a notice is recorded for later delivery
  and nothing is sent ([ADR 0025](../adr/0025-telling-a-poster-their-listing-was-removed.md)).
- **Who writes the text alternative of a photo.** Each photo shown large has one that describes
  it; Post has no field for it.
- **How a member reorders photos.** The first photo is the one shown on the card; Post draws no
  control that moves a photo.
- **What Post shows when a search term of 81 to 100 characters becomes the title,** which takes 80.
- **The longest area a listing may have.** The field shows no limit. (The database keeps an area
  of up to 80 characters, [ADR 0012](../adr/0012-area-without-distance.md).) The city of a new
  church takes up to 80 characters ([ADR 0018](../adr/0018-joining-open-decisions.md)).
- **Splitting the backend.** Both applications use one backend; a separate service for Admin is
  not asked for.
