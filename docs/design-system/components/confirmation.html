Confirmation

What replaces a form once it has done its job: a tick, what happened, what happens next.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

After a listing is posted or a message is sent, the form is replaced, in place, by a confirmation. It tells the truth about what just happened: a new listing waits for review and is not public yet.

Anatomy

  1. BadgeA tick on orange for a listing for rent, on blue for a wanted one, on green in the message box.
  2. Title“Sent for review”.
  3. TextWhat happens next, truthfully.
  4. Main actionBack to Browse.
  5. Second actionPost another.

Sizes

Confirmation has one size.

States

A confirmation is an end state; its buttons are buttons.

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and wraps its text; it has no breakpoint of its own.

Theming

Confirmation reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-success-solid, --color-fg-on-success, --color-rent, --color-fg-on-rent, --color-wanted, --color-fg-on-wanted and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="status", so a screen reader announces it when it replaces the form. Its title is the screen’s <h1> on Post.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Moves to “Back to browsing”.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

The tick is decorative; the title carries the meaning.

Contrast

Tick on orange
Tick on blue
Tick on green
Text

Motion

The badge and text fade in over --duration-base. The prototype’s spring is not used.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Never say a listing is live before it is approved. “Sent for review … It isn’t public yet.”
  • Show the card as it will appear.
  • Offer the next step: “Back to browsing”, “Post another”.
