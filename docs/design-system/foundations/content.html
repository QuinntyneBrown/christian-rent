Overview

The copy is part of the design. It uses the product’s own words, says only what is true, and never sells.

Formulas

  • Button: a verb and its object, naming the person where there is one. “Publish listing”, “Message Dan”.
  • Error on a field: what to do, then the rule. “Enter a city or neighbourhood in Canada, such as ‘Willoughby, Langley’.”
  • Failure: what happened, what is safe, what to do. “Your message wasn’t sent. We couldn’t reach christian.rent. Your message and dates are still here.”
  • Empty: what happened and the one next step. “Nobody has ‘hot air balloon’ yet.” then “Ask for ‘hot air balloon’”.
  • Limit: the count and when it lifts. “You’ve sent 20 messages in the last hour. You can send another at 3:40 p.m.”

Rules

  • Canadian spelling: neighbourhood, colour, counselling.
  • Sentence case. No all-capitals. Curly quotation marks and apostrophes.
  • The wordmark is lowercase “christian.rent”.
  • Nothing about paying, booking, reserving, deposits, reviews or ratings.
  • No reply times, audience sizes or other figures the product does not measure.
  • What a member writes is data: it is shown as text exactly as written.
