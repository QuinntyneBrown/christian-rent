Search bar

Browse’s search: what you are looking for, where, and Search.

Overview

One search bar, on Browse. It finds approved listings by the words in their title, note and area. The list updates 300 ms after typing stops; the button is there for people who expect one.

Anatomy

  1. IconDecorative.
  2. FieldA search input with a hidden label and an example as its placeholder.
  3. AreaThe area searched and how far, as text.
  4. SearchThe plain primary button.

Responsive behaviour

Under 576 px the field, the area and the button stack, and the button is full width. From 576 px they sit in one row with a hairline before the area.

Theming

Search bar reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-bg-surface, --color-border-default, --color-fg-muted, --color-focus-ring, --color-fg-default, --color-fg-subtle and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <form role="search"> holding a native <input type="search">.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter Searches at once.
Esc Clears the field.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

The input’s label, “Search listings”, is visually hidden. The number of results is announced through a polite live region on Browse.

Contrast

Typed term

Motion

Colour and position changes take --duration-fast or --duration-base with --ease-standard. With reduced motion the tokens drop to 0.01 ms, so nothing moves.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Placeholder: “Fellowship hall, guest room, trailer…”. Three real things, never “Search…”.
  • A term is cut to 100 characters and always rendered as text.
  • The area reads “Langley, BC · within 25 km”; distances are in km.
