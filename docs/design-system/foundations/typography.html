Foundation

Typography

Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, names and prices. Instrument Sans for everything that is read.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • Roles
  • Usage
  • Accessibility
  • Tokens

Overview

Two families, both self-hosted from assets/fonts/ under the SIL Open Font License; nothing is loaded from a CDN. Bricolage Grotesque is used large and heavy: headlines, card titles, prices, the wordmark. Instrument Sans carries body text, labels and buttons.

Usage

  • Body text is 16 px and never smaller on a form control. Nothing is under 12 px.
  • Headlines are tight (--letter-spacing-tight); the Browse headline is tighter still (--letter-spacing-display).
  • h1 is 36 px on a phone and 48 px from 768 px; the Browse headline reaches 60 px from 992 px.
  • A listing’s note is set at 18 px with --line-height-relaxed and no wider than 42 rem.
  • Prices, counts and counters use tabular figures.
  • Sentence case everywhere. No all-capitals.

Accessibility

  • Sizes are in rem, so text follows the reader’s setting.
  • At 200% zoom nothing is lost or overlaps: titles wrap, they are not cut.
  • Line height is at least 1.5 for body text.
  • Fonts use font-display: swap, so text is readable before they load.
