Textarea

Several lines of a member’s own words: a message, a listing’s note, a report’s note.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use it wherever a member writes more than a line. Pair it with a counter, because every one has a limit: 1,000 characters for a message or a note, 500 for a report.

Anatomy

  1. Label“Message”, “Anything else?”, “Note”.
  2. BoxAt least three rows; grows by dragging its corner.
  3. CounterCharacters used and the limit, right-aligned.

Variants

With a counter

Textarea

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and wraps its text; it has no breakpoint of its own.

Theming

Textarea reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-fg-default, --color-bg-surface, --color-border-strong, --color-fg-subtle, --color-fg-muted, --color-focus-ring and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <textarea> with a <label>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter Adds a line; it never sends.
Tab Moves on.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

A visible label. The counter is text beside it; when the limit is reached the field’s error says so.

Contrast

Text
Counter
Edge

Motion

Colour and position changes take --duration-fast or --duration-base with --ease-standard. With reduced motion the tokens drop to 0.01 ms, so nothing moves.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • What a member writes is data: it is always rendered as text, never as HTML.
  • A draft opening (“Hi Dan, …”) may be filled in for them; they can change every word.
