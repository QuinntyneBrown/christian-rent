Navigation and page structure

How the screens of the two applications are put together and how a person moves between them.

Overview

christian.rent is two applications: the site, with Browse, Listing, Post and Sign in, and Admin, with the review queue and its own sign-in, each on its own origin. There is no menu because there is nowhere else to go. The logo goes home, Post posts, a card opens its listing, and “All listings” comes back.

When to use

ScreenStructure
BrowseSite header, headline with the two posting buttons, search bar, the switch and kind filter, the grid of cards.
ListingSite header, “All listings” and Report, then the listing with the message box beside it.
PostA plain header with the logo and Close, the sentence, the fields, the action; the live preview beside them.
Sign inSite header with the logo and Post, then the sign-in form.
AdminAdmin navigation, the review queue, the detail panel with its decision bar.
Admin sign-in and forbiddenThe Admin gate: Admin’s ink page around the sign-in form, or the forbidden page.

States

The header’s only state is who is signed in. A person who is not a member sees the same screens and is asked to join when they post, message or report.

Accessibility

  • One <header>, one <main>, one <h1> per screen.
  • A skip link comes first.
  • The switch’s choice and the search term live in the address.
  • Leaving a screen releases what it held: listings, photos, uploads in progress.

Content

  • Screen names are “Browse”, “Listing”, “Post”, “Sign in” and “Admin”.
  • Between the two applications, “Back to site”, “View on site” and “Go to christian.rent” cross to the site’s origin; nothing in the site links to Admin.
