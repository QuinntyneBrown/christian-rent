Radio group

One choice from a short fixed list: the reason for a report.

Overview

Use it when there are two to five choices and seeing them all helps. For the two pairs in Post’s sentence use the word toggle, which is the same control drawn as words.

Anatomy

  1. LegendThe question.
  2. Radio20 px in a 44 px row.
  3. OptionA short phrase.

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and wraps its text; it has no breakpoint of its own.

Theming

Radio group reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-border-strong, --color-bg-surface, --color-accent, --color-fg-on-accent, --color-bg-subtle, --color-border-default and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native radios sharing a name, in a <fieldset> with a <legend>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Moves to the chosen radio, or the first.
↑ ↓ Move the choice.
Space Chooses the focused radio.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

The legend is the group’s name. When nothing is chosen on submit the group is described by its error.

Contrast

Dot on ink
Edge

Motion

Colour and position changes take --duration-fast or --duration-base with --ease-standard. With reduced motion the tokens drop to 0.01 ms, so nothing moves.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • The legend is the question; each option answers it.
  • No option is chosen in advance when the choice is the person’s judgement.
