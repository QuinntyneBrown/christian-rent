Review detail

Admin’s detail panel: everything needed to decide on one item, and the buttons that decide.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

The panel shows a new listing with all its fields and photos, a report with its reason above the listing, or a new member with their church and declaration. The decision bar stays at the bottom: Approve or Remove for something new, Keep or Remove for a report.

Anatomy

  1. Photo, wanted block or memberWhat the item is.
  2. TitleAn h2.
  3. PriceBeside the title.
  4. FactsPosted by, church, when, where, member since.
  5. Approve or KeepGreen.
  6. RemoveQuiet red; opens the confirmation.

Responsive behaviour

The panel does not exist under 768 px: an admin decides on a tablet or a computer. From 768 px its facts are in two columns and its decision bar sticks to the bottom.

Theming

Review detail reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-danger-bg, --color-danger-fg, --color-bg-surface, --color-wanted, --color-fg-on-wanted, --color-border-default and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-label="Detail">; facts are a <dl>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Approve or Keep, then Remove, then “View on site”.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

Buttons say what they do. The report’s reason is text, never colour alone, and never says who reported.

Contrast

Text on Admin’s canvas
Fact labels
Report reason
Approve

Motion

Colour and position changes take --duration-fast or --duration-base with --ease-standard. With reduced motion the tokens drop to 0.01 ms, so nothing moves.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • “Approve” for a new listing or member; “Keep listing” for a report; “Remove” always.
  • A report reads “Reported as a scam by a member, 40 min ago”, then the note in quotation marks.
  • Exact times here: “Jun 8, 2026, 9:12 a.m.”
