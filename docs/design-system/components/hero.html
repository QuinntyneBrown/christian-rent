Browse headline

The top of Browse: what christian.rent is, and the two buttons that lead straight to posting.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Used once, on Browse. It says what the place is in one line and offers both ways in: you have something, or you are looking for something.

Anatomy

  1. HeadlineThe screen’s h1, display face.
  2. Accent lineIn --color-fg-rent-display.
  3. LeadOne sentence, with the area.
  4. “I have something to rent”Orange; opens Post set to “have”.
  5. “I’m looking for something”Outline; opens Post set to “need”.

Variants

Rent it from your church family.

Halls, rooms, vans and tools, shared between believers near Langley, BC. Or ask for what you need.

Sizes

Browse headline has one size.

States

Not interactive itself; its two buttons are buttons.

Responsive behaviour

The headline is 36 px under 576 px, 48 px from 576 px and 60 px from 992 px. From 992 px the buttons sit to the right of the text, on its baseline; under that they are below it and wrap.

Theming

Browse headline reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-fg-rent-display, --color-fg-muted. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section> named by the screen’s <h1>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab The two buttons, in order.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

The buttons’ text is exact and required: “I have something to rent” and “I’m looking for something”.

Contrast

Headline
Accent line (large text)
Lead

Motion

None.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • “Rent it from your church family.”
  • The lead names real things and the area: “Halls, rooms, vans and tools, shared between believers near Langley, BC. Or ask for what you need.”
