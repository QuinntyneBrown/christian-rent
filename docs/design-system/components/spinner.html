Spinner

A small turning ring for a wait that has no shape.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use it where there is nothing to draw a skeleton of: the next page of listings arriving at the end of Browse, a photo uploading. A busy button draws its own.

Anatomy

  1. RingA hairline ring with one ink quarter.

Variants

With its text
HTML
<div class="feed-status" role="status" style="margin: 0; padding: 0;"><span class="spinner" aria-hidden="true"></span><p>Loading more listings…</p></div>

Sizes

20 px and 40 px.

States

Not interactive: it has no states.

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and wraps its text; it has no breakpoint of its own.

Theming

Spinner reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-border-default, --color-accent. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="status" with a name, or decorative beside visible text in a status.

Keyboard

KeyAction
NoneNot focusable: it is read in document order.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

“Uploading photo 2”, “Loading more listings…”.

Contrast

Ring

Motion

One turn every 0.8 s. Reduced motion stops it; the text still says what is happening.

Touch

Nothing to press.

Content

  • Always say what is being waited for.
