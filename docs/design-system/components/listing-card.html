Listing card

One listing on Browse: a photo for something for rent, a blue card with no photo for something wanted.

Overview

The card is the product’s main object. A listing for rent leads with its photo. A wanted listing has no photo, so its title becomes the picture: white on blue, with when it is needed. Both then give the price, the area and the church. The same card is the live preview on Post and the confirmation after posting.

Anatomy

  1. Kind tag“Space” or “Thing”, on the photo.
  2. Photo4:3, --radius-lg, its space reserved before it loads.
  3. TitleBricolage Grotesque, semibold, up to 80 characters, wrapping.
  4. Price“$45 / hour”; “Free”; absent when a wanted listing gives no budget.
  5. AreaA city or neighbourhood and the distance in km.
  6. ChurchThe church of the member who posted.

Responsive behaviour

The grid is one column under 576 px, two from 576 px, three from 768 px and four from 1200 px. The row gap is 40 px; the column gap 24 px.

Theming

Listing card reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-bg-surface-sunken, --color-fg-muted, --color-fg-default, --color-wanted, --color-fg-on-wanted. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

One link wrapping the whole card, inside a list item of the listing list. On Post and in the confirmation it is a plain block, not a link. Its title is an h2 on Browse and in the confirmation; in Post’s preview, which sits under the heading “How it’ll look”, it is a paragraph (ADR 0019).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Moves from card to card.
Enter Opens the listing.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

The link’s name is its text: title, price, area, church. The photo’s alt describes what it shows. A wanted card says “Wanted” in words; the blue is never the only signal.

Contrast

Title
Price unit, area, church
Title on a wanted card

Motion

On hover the card rises 4 px over --duration-base. Reduced motion removes it.

Touch

The whole card is the target.

Content

  • Price: “$25 / day”, “Up to $120 / day”, “Free”. Cents only when they are not zero. Periods are hour, day, week, month.
  • Area: “Walnut Grove, Langley · 5 km”. Never a street address.
  • The wanted tag reads “Wanted · thing” or “Wanted · space”.
  • An empty preview shows “Your listing title” and “Price”, never “undefined” or “$”.
