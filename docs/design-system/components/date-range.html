Date range

A start and an end date, sent with a message about a listing for rent or given as a listing’s “when”.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

The dates describe what two people will agree; they reserve nothing. There is no calendar of availability on christian.rent, and the control must never look like one.

Anatomy

  1. FromA native date field.
  2. ToOn or after From.
  3. SummaryThe dates written out with the month as a word, so they cannot be misread.

Responsive behaviour

The two fields sit side by side down to 360 px and stack under it.

Theming

Date range reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-fg-muted. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Two native <input type="date"> in a <fieldset> with a legend.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Moves through day, month and year.
↑ ↓ Change the focused part.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

Each field is labelled “From” and “To”; the group’s legend is “Dates”. One error describes both fields.

Contrast

Summary
Error

Motion

Colour and position changes take --duration-fast or --duration-base with --ease-standard. With reduced motion the tokens drop to 0.01 ms, so nothing moves.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Dates are written “Sat, Jun 13, 2026”: the month as a word, the year always.
  • A range reads “Jul 14 to 18, 2026”.
  • Errors name the date to choose: “Choose Jun 13, 2026 or later.” In the message box the summary adds how many days the dates span, counting both ends: “Sat, Jun 13, 2026 · one day”, “Jun 13 to 15, 2026 · 3 days”; a start in the past reads “The start date is in the past. Choose Jun 8, 2026 or later.”
