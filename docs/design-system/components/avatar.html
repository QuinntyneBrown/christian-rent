Avatar

A member’s initials on a pale tile.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

christian.rent has no profile photos. Initials stand beside a name to give it a face; they are decoration and are never the only way a person is identified.

Anatomy

  1. TileA circle in one of three pale tints, with ink initials in the display face.

Variants

DWRMML
Three tints
HTML
<span class="avatar avatar--sun" aria-hidden="true">DW</span><span class="avatar avatar--sky" aria-hidden="true">RM</span><span class="avatar avatar--leaf" aria-hidden="true">ML</span>

Sizes

SKSKSK
32, 40 and 56 px.

States

Not interactive: it has no states.

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and wraps its text; it has no breakpoint of its own.

Theming

Avatar reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-accent-subtle, --color-fg-accent, --color-bg-surface, --color-avatar-sky, --color-fg-on-avatar, --color-avatar-sun and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Decorative: aria-hidden="true", because the name is always beside it.

Keyboard

KeyAction
NoneNot focusable: it is read in document order.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

None; the name next to it is the label.

Contrast

Initials on orange
Initials on blue
Initials on green

Motion

None.

Touch

Nothing to press.

Content

  • Two letters: the first letters of the first two words of the name, skipping “Pastor”, “Dr.” and “The”.
  • A member keeps the same tint everywhere.
