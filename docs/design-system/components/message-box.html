Message box

The panel beside a listing that sends a message to the person who posted it.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

This is how a rental is arranged: a member writes to the person who posted, and the two agree the details together. The box shows the price, takes dates when the listing is for rent, takes the message, and is replaced by a confirmation once it is sent. It takes no payment and reserves nothing.

Anatomy

  1. Eyebrow“Rent for” or “They’ll pay”.
  2. PriceDisplay face, 36 px, with “CAD / hour” beside it.
  3. DatesOnly for a listing for rent.
  4. MessageUp to 1,000 characters.
  5. SendOrange for a listing for rent, blue for a wanted one; names the person.
  6. Fine printAlways present.

Sizes

Message box has one size.

States

A wanted listing that gave no price has no head: the box starts at the message field; see listing/no-price. On a member’s own listing the box keeps its price and says “This is your listing.” with no form; see listing/own. The dates are optional and start empty.

Invalid, failed and rate-limited states put an alert at the top of the form and keep the message and dates; see listing/invalid, failed and limited. On the member’s own listing the box has no form.

Responsive behaviour

From 768 px the box sits to the right of the listing, 380 px wide at most, and stays in view as the listing scrolls. Under 768 px it is below the listing, full width.

Theming

Message box reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-default, --color-fg-muted, --color-bg-subtle. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section> with a heading, holding a form.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Dates, message, send.
Enter In a date field, sends; in the message, adds a line.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

The section’s heading is visually hidden: “Message Dan about this listing”. The confirmation is a role="status", so it is announced when it replaces the form.

Contrast

Price
Send, for rent
Send, wanted
Fine print

Motion

The confirmation fades in over --duration-base.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Button: “Message Dan” for a listing for rent, “Offer to help Marcus” for a wanted one. Titles such as “Pastor” and “Dr.” are dropped.
  • Fine print: “No payment happens here. You’ll agree the details together.”
  • Sent: “Sent to Dan”, then what happens next. Never a reply time and never an email address.
