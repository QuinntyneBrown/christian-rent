Tag

A short label: what kind of listing, or what kind of item in the queue.

Overview

A tag names a category in a word or two. It is never pressed; a filter is a kind filter.

Anatomy

  1. Pill24 px tall, 12 px semibold text, a tint that goes with the word.

Sizes

ThingFor rent · space
24 px, and 28 px with tag--lg on the Listing screen.

States

Not interactive: it has no states.

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and wraps its text; it has no breakpoint of its own.

Theming

Tag reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-bg-subtle, --color-fg-default, --color-wanted-subtle, --color-fg-wanted, --color-danger-bg, --color-danger-fg and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.
