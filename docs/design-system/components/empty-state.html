Empty and error states

What a screen shows when there is nothing to show: nothing yet, nothing found, nothing loaded, not found, not allowed.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Every one says what happened in a sentence and offers one way forward. The header, the search bar and the switch stay where they are. The rules that choose between them are in the empty and error states pattern.

Anatomy

  1. TitleWhat happened, in the member’s terms; names the search term.
  2. TextOne sentence on what to do.
  3. ActionOne filled button.
  4. Second way (optional)A ghost button.

Sizes

Empty and error states has one size.

States

These are states of a screen, not interactive themselves; their buttons are buttons.

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and wraps its text; it has no breakpoint of its own.

Theming

Empty and error states reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-accent-subtle, --color-fg-accent, --color-fg-muted, --color-danger-bg, --color-danger-icon, --color-success-bg and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section> with a heading at the level the list’s heading would have. Not-found and forbidden screens make it the <h1>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab Moves to the action.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

A failure is also sent to the screen’s live region.

Contrast

Title
Text
Error icon

Motion

None.

Touch

Every interactive part is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-min). Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Nothing found: “Nobody has ‘hot air balloon’ yet.” then “Ask for ‘hot air balloon’”.
  • Nothing yet: “Nothing is posted near Langley yet.” with the two buttons that lead to posting.
  • Not loaded: “We couldn’t load the listings.” then “Try again”.
  • Not found: “This listing isn’t here.” It reveals nothing about the listing.
  • Queue empty: “All caught up”.
  • Detail not loaded: “We couldn’t load this item.” then “Try again”.
  • Item gone: “This item is no longer waiting.” with the alert icon and no danger tint, since nothing failed.
