Foundation

Motion

Quick and quiet. Motion confirms a press or shows where something came from, and it can always be turned off.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • Durations and easings
  • Usage
  • Accessibility
  • Tokens

Overview

Nothing on christian.rent moves to entertain. A button gives under the finger, a card lifts under the pointer, a confirmation fades in where the form was, a dialog rises.

Durations and easings

TokenUsed for
--duration-fastHover, press, colour.
--duration-baseA card lifting, the switch’s pill, a confirmation fading in, a toast rising.
--duration-slowA dialog opening.
--duration-deliberateNothing shorter than a progress or shimmer cycle.
--ease-standardThe prototype’s “snap”: fast out, gentle stop. The default.
--ease-enter, --ease-exitThings arriving and leaving.
--ease-springNot used: the prototype’s bouncing tick was replaced by a fade.

Usage

  • Animate opacity and transform only.
  • Nothing animates on page load except the skeleton’s shimmer.
  • Every timer, listener and animation frame a component starts is stopped when the component is destroyed.

Accessibility

When a person asks for reduced motion, the duration tokens drop to 0.01 ms and the shimmer and spinner stop. Components need no rule of their own; nothing beyond a short fade remains (L2-032).
