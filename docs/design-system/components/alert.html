Alert

A message inside the page, at the place it is about.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use an alert when something about this form or this panel needs saying and must stay: a request failed, a limit was reached, an item was already decided. It sits at the top of the thing it concerns and keeps whatever the member typed.

Anatomy

  1. IconWith the title, carries the tone without colour.
  2. TitleWhat happened.
  3. TextWhat is safe and what to do.

Variants

You can post again tomorrow at 9:15 a.m.

Sizes

Alert has one size.

States

An alert is shown or not; it has no interactive states.

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and wraps its text; it has no breakpoint of its own.

Theming

Alert reads only semantic tokens, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. The colour tokens it reads are --color-info-bg, --color-info-fg, --color-info-border, --color-info-icon, --color-success-bg, --color-success-fg and others listed under Tokens. To restyle it, change those tokens; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="alert" when it appears in response to an action, so it is announced; plain content when it is there from the start.

Keyboard

KeyAction
NoneNot focusable: it is read in document order.

Focus

The shared focus ring: a 2 px outline in --color-focus-ring, offset 2 px, never removed.

Labelling

The title says what happened without the icon or the tint.

Contrast

Danger
Warning
Info
Success

Motion

None.

Touch

Any link or button inside is 44 px tall.

Content

  • What happened, then what is safe, then what to do: “Your message wasn’t sent. We couldn’t reach christian.rent. Your message and dates are still here.”
  • A limit says when it lifts: “You can send another at 3:40 p.m.”
  • No codes, no “Error:”, no blame.
