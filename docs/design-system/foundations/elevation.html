Foundation

Elevation

Flat by default. A soft shadow lifts the two panels a member acts in; a deeper one lifts a dialog.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • Shadow levels
  • Layers
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Most of christian.rent is flat: white on white, divided by hairlines. Shadow is kept for what floats over the page or asks to be used: the search bar, the message box, a dialog, a toast.

Usage

  • Cards on Browse have no shadow and no border; the photo is the edge.
  • In the dark theme shadows are stronger and raised surfaces are lighter than the page.
  • A component never sets a raw z-index.
